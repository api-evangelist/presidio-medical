# Presidio Medical

> Privately held, clinical-stage medical device company in South San Francisco, California,
> founded in 2017 by Kenneth Wu and led by CEO and Chairman Michael Onuscheck. Presidio Medical
> is developing an implantable Ultra Low Frequency (ULF) neuromodulation platform to treat
> diseases of undesired neural activity, with a first indication in chronic nociceptive low back
> pain. The therapy delivers ultra low frequency current through an epidural spinal cord
> stimulation (SCS) lead to reversibly inhibit pain-signaling neurons via sodium channel
> inactivation — a non-opioid alternative to conventional SCS. The company raised a $72M Series C
> in 2023 led by Deerfield Management and holds FDA IDE approval for the global pivotal FULFILL
> randomized controlled trial in the United States and Australia.
